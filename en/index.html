How much ARR still depends on maintenance contracts

In established software companies, some customers often still have a perpetual licence with a maintenance contract alongside the subscriptions. Whether a buyer accepts that maintenance as recurring revenue is a valuation question. How it works

You don't need any new software for this. I work with read access to HubSpot and exports from your systems.

  1. Intro call about the occasion, the date and your systems.

  2. Non-disclosure agreement, read access to HubSpot, exports from ERP and accounting.

  3. I run the analysis and present the list of findings to you within two to three weeks.

  4. We go through the results together, and then you get all documents for the data room.

I'll give you the price after the intro call. It depends on how many contracts and systems are involved.

For due diligence advisors and investors: If an advisor is already engaged, I support their work. Every number can be traced back to its source document and taken over as a file. The judgement stays with the advisor. CogniCore is not an audit firm.
