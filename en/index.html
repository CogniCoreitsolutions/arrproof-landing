Where ARRproof fits into a transaction

Before the other side starts asking questions, your numbers are backed by documents and the gaps are known. If you start early, you have time to close them.

  1. TodayThe date is set or on the horizon.
  2. ARRproofI analyse HubSpot, invoices, bank and books and present the list of findings.
  3. Clean-upWhatever can be fixed beforehand gets fixed, directly in HubSpot by us if you like.
  4. Data roomNumbers and exports are ready, each one traceable to its source document.
  5. Due diligenceThe other side's reviewers work with the same data.
  6. SigningOpen data questions are settled by then.

How it works

You don't need any new software for this. I work with read access to HubSpot and exports from your systems.

  1. Intro call about the occasion, the date and your systems.

  2. Non-disclosure agreement, read access to HubSpot, exports from ERP and accounting.

  3. I run the analysis and present the list of findings to you.

  4. We go through the results together, and then you get all documents for the data room.

I'll give you the price after the intro call. It depends on how many contracts and systems are involved.

For due diligence advisors and investors

If an advisor is already engaged, I support their work. Every number can be traced back to its source document and taken over as a file. The judgement stays with the advisor. CogniCore is not an audit firm.
