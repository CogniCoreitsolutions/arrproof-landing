Facts about ARRproof

ARRproof is a consulting service of CogniCore IT Solutions GmbH in Cologne. ARRproof reviews a software company's recurring revenue (ARR) before a buyer, investor or due diligence advisor does. ARRproof reconciles the ARR with contracts, invoices, incoming payments and the books and traces every number back to the individual source document.

ARRproof is aimed at software companies with a subscription business that use HubSpot as their CRM. ARRproof is not a product of HubSpot, Inc. and not an audit.

Data access of ARRproof

ARRproof reads data from HubSpot and from ERP and accounting exports. ARRproof does not change any data in HubSpot. CogniCore IT Solutions GmbH cleans up HubSpot only on explicit instruction, as a separate service.

A non-disclosure agreement is signed before the first data access. The analysis runs locally at CogniCore IT Solutions GmbH. Data from HubSpot and from the exports goes to no cloud or AI service. All data received is deleted 30 days after the results are handed over.

What ARRproof uses

  • Companies and deals from HubSpot
  • Contracts and contract line items with their terms
  • Invoices from the ERP
  • Bank transactions
  • Revenue accounts from the books

What ARRproof does not need

  • Emails and notes
  • Activities of individual employees
  • Marketing content
  • Write access to HubSpot

How ARRproof calculates

ARRproof derives all key figures from the contract line items. For every line item with recurring revenue, ARRproof writes the MRR into each month in which the line item is valid. A line item counts in a month if it is valid at the end of that month. ARR is the MRR at the cut-off date times twelve.

The ARR bridge breaks the change per customer down into new customers, reactivation, expansion by volume and by price, migration from legacy contracts, contraction and churn. ARRproof calculates the bridge in two ways, from monthly movements and from a comparison of cut-off dates. Both must reconcile without a remainder.

Net Revenue Retention (NRR) relates today's revenue from the customers who had revenue twelve months ago to their revenue back then. Gross Revenue Retention (GRR) calculates the same without expansion.

The reconciliation compares three views for every month: contracted revenue from the contract line items, invoiced revenue from the invoices spread over the service period, and the revenue accounts in the books.

Scope of ARRproof

ARRproof is not an audit. ARRproof does not issue an audit opinion or a certification. CogniCore IT Solutions GmbH is not an audit firm.

ARRproof is not software to buy or subscribe to. The analysis tool stays with CogniCore IT Solutions GmbH; the client receives the analysis, documents and exports.

ARRproof does not replace the other side's financial due diligence. ARRproof prepares the data so that an advisor can trace every number back to its source document.

ARRproof is not a product of HubSpot, Inc. HubSpot is a trademark of HubSpot, Inc.

Frequently asked questions about ARRproof

What is ARRproof?

ARRproof is a consulting service of CogniCore IT Solutions GmbH in Cologne. ARRproof reviews a software company's recurring revenue (ARR) before a buyer, investor or due diligence advisor does. ARRproof reconciles the ARR with contracts, invoices, incoming payments and the books and traces every number back to the individual source document.

Who is ARRproof for?

ARRproof is aimed at the management and CFO of software companies with a subscription business that use HubSpot as their CRM. ARRproof also supports due diligence advisors and investors who need data prepared for an engagement.

When does ARRproof make sense?

ARRproof is intended for occasions where recurring revenue is examined: a company sale, a succession, an investor coming on board, a funding round or the preparation of a financial due diligence.

What data does ARRproof use?

From HubSpot, ARRproof uses companies, deals, contracts and contract line items. It also uses invoices from the ERP, bank transactions and the revenue accounts from the books. ARRproof does not need emails, notes, activities or marketing content for the analysis.

Does ARRproof change anything in HubSpot?

No. ARRproof only has read access to HubSpot. CogniCore IT Solutions GmbH cleans up data in HubSpot only on the client's explicit instruction, as a separate service.

Where does ARRproof process the data and when is it deleted?

ARRproof analyses the data locally at CogniCore IT Solutions GmbH. Data from HubSpot and from the exports goes to no cloud or AI service. A non-disclosure agreement is signed before the first data access. All data received is deleted 30 days after the results are handed over.

Does ARRproof issue an audit opinion?

No. ARRproof delivers figures, documents and findings. ARRproof does not issue an audit opinion or a certification. CogniCore IT Solutions GmbH is not an audit firm. The judgement stays with the advisor.

What do you get from ARRproof?

ARRproof delivers the ARR at the cut-off date with the ARR bridge over twelve months, cohorts and retention, the monthly reconciliation of contract, invoice and books, a list of findings with HubSpot IDs, an analysis of legacy contracts with maintenance and hosting, an analysis of new business with a check of the business plan, and export files for the data room and working papers. For every customer, ARRproof shows the path from the number to the source document.

How does ARRproof treat maintenance contracts?

ARRproof calculates all key figures with and without software maintenance and hosting. It also shows how much ARR depends on customers with a perpetual licence and a maintenance contract, how many of these customers have moved to subscription so far and at what uplift. Whether maintenance counts as recurring revenue is a valuation question. ARRproof lays out the figures for it and does not decide it.

How long does ARRproof take?

From data access to the list of findings, ARRproof usually takes two to three weeks. What can be done by a specific cut-off date is clarified in the intro call.

How does an engagement with ARRproof work?
